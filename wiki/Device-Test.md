# Device test

Settings → **Help & about** → **Device test** checks what Hugminn can use on your handheld. It is the quickest way to find out whether your device is supported – and to tell us.

1. **Automatic part (about 10 seconds):** device and screen (resolution, aspect ratio, refresh rates); permissions; the handheld's own features (performance and fan profiles, button layout, triggers, stick LEDs, charging separation, LED driver); live values (CPU, GPU, temperatures, battery, fan speed, FPS); capture (video encoder for 1080p/60 FPS, game sound); controllers (built-in, external, battery).
2. **Buttons:** press each button once – A, B, X, Y, shoulder buttons, triggers, stick clicks, Start, Select, volume, Home and Back. Ends by itself when all are pressed, or after 30 seconds.
3. **Stick LEDs:** the sticks light up red – did you see it? Afterwards your colours come back.
4. **Result:** everything grouped by area with ✓ (works), ⚠ (limited) or ✗ (not available) and a short note, e.g. "Reports no battery level".

**Copy** puts the result on the clipboard; **Report on GitHub** opens a filled-in [device report](https://github.com/Ninte86hl/Hugminn/issues/new?template=device-report.yml) in your browser – device, version, root and the whole result are already filled in, you only add what you noticed yourself and send it. No names, Wi-Fi names or other private data are included.

Owners of devices that aren't tested yet (Pocket 4/4 Pro, Pocket 5, G2, AYN Odin 2 …): please run it and send the result – it helps a lot.
