# External controller

Hugminn works with Bluetooth and USB controllers such as an 8BitDo.

## Home button
The controller's Home/Guide button works like the handheld's Home button: press, double press and hold do the same as on the device. It can be turned off in the [settings](Settings) → *Home button* → *Home button on a controller*, for example if an emulator uses the Guide button itself.

## Turning off the built-in controls
Hold the **Controller style** tile in the [Control Center](Control-Center) (or press □ / X on it) and choose **Built-in controls off**. The handheld's own buttons and sticks stop working and only the external controller is used – the same as Retroid's own quick setting.

- **With a controller connected:** the built-in controls stay off until you switch them back on, or until the controller disconnects.
- **Without a controller yet:** the Control Center closes and a countdown in the middle of the screen asks you to connect one. If none connects within 30 seconds, the built-in controls come back on by themselves.
- **Controller disconnected:** the built-in controls come back on automatically, with the layout you had before (Modern or Retro).

## If no button responds
Touch always works:
- In the Control Center, **swipe down from the top edge** to open Android's quick settings.
- The **Control Center** quick settings tile and the [shortcuts](Shortcuts) open the Control Center without any button.

## Battery
The [Bluetooth](Control-Center) tile and the device battery tile show a controller's charge if it reports one. Not every controller does: an 8BitDo Ultimate in Xbox mode, for example, reports no battery level to Android, so no app can show it.
