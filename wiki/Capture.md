# Capture

The **Capture** tile in the [Control Center](Control-Center) takes screenshots and videos of your game – like the capture button on a console.

## Quick use
- **A on the tile:** screenshot of the game. The Control Center closes first, so only the game is in the picture.
- **While a video is recording:** the tile shows "● 1:24"; A on the tile stops it.
- **X on the tile:** the Capture menu.

## The Capture menu
Two tabs, switched with **L1 / R1**; values change with **◀ ▶** on the D-pad.

**Take:** Screenshot · Screenshot in 3 s · Record video / Stop video · Video in 3 s · Save the last 30 s (when *Keep recording* is on) · View last capture.

**Settings:**
| Category | Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Picture | Resolution | 1080p, 720p |
| | Frame rate | 60, 30 FPS |
| | Maximum length | No limit, 5 s, 10 s, 15 s, 30 s, 1, 3, 5, 10 min – the video stops and saves itself |
| Sound | Record | Game sound · Game sound + microphone · Microphone only · No sound |
| While recording | Indicator | Stick LEDs pulse red (even when they are switched off – they go on just for the recording) · red dot with time on screen · both · nothing |
| | Screen off saves the video | on/off – turning the screen off stops and saves cleanly |
| Last seconds | Keep recording | records in the background all the time and keeps only the last 15/30/60 s |
| | Clip length | 15, 30, 60 s |

## Save the last seconds
With *Keep recording* on, Hugminn always keeps the last seconds in memory. Something great happens? Choose *Save the last 30 s* (or put it on a [Home button](Home-Button) gesture) and exactly that moment is saved as a clip – even if you never pressed record. It uses some battery and Android shows its recording icon the whole time, so it is off by default.

## Home button actions
Three actions can go on press, double press or hold: **Screenshot**, **Record video / stop** and **Save the last seconds**.

## Where the files go
Screenshots: *Pictures/Screenshots*. Videos and clips: *Movies/Hugminn*. The first time you open one, Android asks which app should play it – choose your gallery and *Always*.

## Good to know
- Android shows a recording icon and a notification "Hugminn is recording" while capturing – that is required and can't be hidden.
- Game sound is captured directly from Android. A few apps don't allow it; their videos stay silent. Emulators normally allow it.
- Without root, Android asks "Start recording?" each time and asks once for the microphone.
