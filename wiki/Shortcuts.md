# Shortcuts

Besides the Home button, the Control Center (Huginn, red) and the game switcher (Muninn, blue) can be opened with a tap.

## Quick settings tile
Pull down Android's quick settings, tap the pencil and drag the **Control Center** tile into the active tiles. Tapping it closes the quick settings and opens the Control Center. A long press opens Hugminn's settings.

## Long press on the app icon
Long press the Hugminn icon in your launcher: **Control Center** and **Game switcher** appear. Tap one to open it, or drag it onto the home screen.

## Home screen and frontends (e.g. Cocoon)
Hugminn settings → **Shortcuts** → *Add* puts the Control Center or the game switcher on the home screen of your **default launcher**.

**Cocoon:** set Cocoon as your default launcher first (Android settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app), then tap *Add*. Cocoon adds the shortcut to its library like a game.

## As separate apps
Hugminn settings → **Shortcuts** → *Show as separate apps*: "Hugminn Control Center" and "Hugminn Game switcher" appear in the app list. Meant for frontends that only list apps; off by default.
